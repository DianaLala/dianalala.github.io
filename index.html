About me

Design as a tool for dignity

I'm a Kampala-based graphic designer, co-creation facilitator, and visual storyteller with over 15 years of experience working at the intersection of design and social justice.

Specialised in inclusive communication tools for marginalised communities — refugees, women, persons with disabilities, and LGBTQ+ communities.

Co-founder and Creative Lead of Urithi Studio, developing behaviour change communication strategies and social innovation tools.

Experienced in designing for diverse, international audiences across Uganda, Rwanda, Zambia, Botswana, Mozambique, the Netherlands, Germany, and the UK.

Proficient in Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign), Figma, and Canva. Certified in IDEO's Human-Centered Design approach.
